The Atelier of
Silent Luxury

Born in Istanbul in 2021, SAN DUKHAR bridges centuries of Anatolian craftsmanship with a contemporary vision of understated elegance.

"Anyone can imitate it, but no one can surpass it."

SAN DUKHAR was born from a profound respect for nature's most exquisite materials.

Every stitch, every cut, and every polish is performed by a master artisan.

— AbdurRohman Musaev, Founder & Creative Director

Our Journey

2021
The First Atelier

SAN DUKHAR opens its first workshop in Zeytinburnu, Istanbul.

2022
Bespoke Program Launch

The Bespoke Atelier is established.

2023
The Sovereign Collection

The iconic Sovereign Collection debuts — instantly winning acclaim among connoisseurs of exotic leather worldwide.

2024
Global Recognition

We become more widely known, and trust in SAN DUKHAR continues to grow among clients around the world.

2025
New Boutique

We open a new store in the heart of Istanbul, expanding the atelier's presence.

2026
A New Chapter

The house unveils its renewed digital presence.
